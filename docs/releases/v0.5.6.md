# Copicu v0.5.6: Sign in on another PC

Link another PC with the same admitted Google account without asking an existing
PC for approval or entering a recovery code. Every linked PC has the same account
access; folder connections and send/receive settings remain local and explicit.

Copicu Sharing now manages content keys. Payloads remain encrypted in transit and
storage, but the service operator can decrypt them; this mode is not end-to-end
encryption. The service signs its encryption identity and key packages, and the
Windows client verifies them against its pinned issuer.

Update and open an existing PC first to migrate keys for previously shared
clipboards. Existing connections, copies and keys are preserved. The service keeps
legacy device packages during migration; update all PCs before creating new shared
clipboards or rotating their keys. Signing in does not send existing history or
enable Windows clipboard updates or Actions.

This release also fixes concurrent reads of locally protected credentials: status
and background synchronization can read together while writes and deletion stay
blocked.

## Verification and scope

The candidate source passed 392 Rust tests (one benchmark ignored), 59 Bun tests
with 662 assertions, and 32 renderer cases across desktop and narrow layouts.
Coverage includes legacy migration, account separation, later devices, text/PNG,
key rotation, signed recipient tampering and backup/restore with the saved secrets.

Five Windows UI/host cases passed with three synthetic profiles and a real local
OIDC/PKCE provider. The final signed installer separately passed five native
payload checks, including Google sign-in and custody messaging without device
approval controls. Its x64 GUI payload, resources, updater signature and trusted
comment were verified; no visual-test bridge is shipped.

The service is deployed with its original issuer, device approval disabled and a
verified backup/restore including the custody secret. Unauthenticated private
routes still return 401.

Installer SHA256:
`C6BFB4F69A87AE7A02BDCD67F0905DC3012D146AFEB60BF9A109C34A626DEB32`.

The service retains its existing Google account admission. Opening Google Audience
and admission to all verified accounts is a separate change. Synthetic acceptance
does not establish a new test on two physical PCs.
